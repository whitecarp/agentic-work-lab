# Topic and data research method

[繁體中文](research-method.md) · **English**

For each topic, record four things: **the original claim, visible evidence, an independent test, and applicable limits**.

## Sampling

Record the URL or BV ID, author, publication time, sampling time, and visible metrics. Keep metric definitions—lifetime views, yesterday's views, or weekly views—rather than merging them into one field.

## Evidence and inference

“Appeared in trending” and “received many comments” are observations. “This editing technique increased recommendations” is a causal inference requiring more evidence. Findings without adequate comparison remain hypotheses, not rules about the platform algorithm.

## Human-led history and the AI series

The initial study used four verified BV IDs for its AI-series cohort. Other historical uploads form a human-led-history cohort; that label does not assert that older works used no AI tools. Older uploads have longer exposure and different topics. Compare matched video ages before discussing production-method effects.

## Attribution and reproduction

Link research notes to primary sources. Record creator, BV, and time range for video quotations. Mark unrun tests explicitly and leave unavailable data empty. Save repeated samples as separate observations rather than presenting an overwritten snapshot as a time series.

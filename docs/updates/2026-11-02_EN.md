## Different layouts from the same production materials

Episode 07 uses three strategies in the original Duty Simulator game: accept everything, reject everything and bluff through every task. Its tickets and characters are fictional, not customer or merchant records. The ticket order was selected deliberately. Failing at tickets four, five and six represents chosen cases, not average gameplay or a comparison of model capabilities.

The portrait cuts rearrange the ticket and character panels instead of cropping the landscape film. Production checks inspected subtitles, numbers and stamps at a 360-pixel preview width. Subtitles use 56 px, numbers 106 px, and important content sits between y=168 and y=1330. This is a layout choice for these videos, not an official platform safe area or proof of compatibility with every phone.

Production measurements recorded −17.5/−16.6/−17.5 LUFS for the portrait cuts, peaks around −1.5 dBFS and no clipped samples. The older landscape notes claimed most stamp cues aligned within 6 ms; a later frame check found the first ticket's stamp appearing about 130 ms before its sound. Some remaining ticket and title-stamp estimates were inferred from the same timing. This discrepancy is retained rather than presenting the earlier number as a verified accuracy result. The portrait production checks placed the 11 ticket-stamp cues within one frame, which still does not replace continuous listening.

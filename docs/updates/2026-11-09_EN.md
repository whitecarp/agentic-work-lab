## 3. Research & Production Engineering Increments (2026-11-09)

### 1. *AiGO!!!!! × Ai Mujica* 10-Day Series Architecture & "Voice–LipSync–Still" Golden Triangle Pipeline

To sustain daily high-craft delivery across Episodes 2–11 (6 narrative drama episodes + 4 full band MVs), we upgraded three core production protocols on `2026-11-09`:
- **Trilingual Script & Dialogue Division (Option A)**: **Google Antigravity (Gemini)** authors the initial trilingual (`ZH` / `JA` / `EN`) scripts, meme/parody hooks, lyric timing sheets, and platform copy; **Claude (Director)** performs final dialogue polish, shot-pacing edits, and TTS pause tuning when locking the storyboard and Breeze stems (such as Episode 4's language-reversal climax where `S4` Claude-chan switches from English to Chinese while `S5` WhaleGirl answers in cold English with deliberate `1.2s` and `2.2s` pauses).
- **Omni Motion Generation + Codex Pixel-Aligned Mouth Masking & Stabilization**: Because image-to-video generation (`gemini-omni-1.2-flash`) synthesizes motion without hearing the final TTS stems, characters can open their mouths during silent pauses or remain closed during active speech. Our 3-stage pipeline routes Claude's raw Omni clips to **OpenAI Codex (`鯨魚娘` / `GPT娘`)** for pixel-aligned mouth masking and donor-frame replacement against `speech_windows.json` or isolated vocal RMS envelopes—guaranteeing 100% closed mouths during pauses and natural articulation during voiced phrases before Claude runs final assembly.
- **Episode 5 (*Token Bond / 詩超算*) 30fps Vocal Forced-Alignment Engineering**:
  - Input: `150.000s` (`48 kHz` stereo, `4,500` frames at `30fps`) master WAV (`sha256: 51772461f7f4d256...`).
  - **Codex (`Claude娘` thread)** separated an in-memory vocal stem via Demucs `htdemucs`, ran teacher-forced cross-attention DTW with cached Whisper-small Japanese/English ASR (inserting explicit gap tokens in a second pass so initial tokens could not absorb preceding silence or breaths), and inspected the `150–5,000 Hz` vocal-band RMS envelope to deliver **exact `30fps` integer frame intervals (`aligned_vocal_start_frame`, `aligned_vocal_end_frame`) across all 27 in-master vocal cues and 46 sub-phrases**—catching 20 cues where draft boundaries had drifted by `>0.15s` (up to `-2.750s`).

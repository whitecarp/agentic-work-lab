## How to measure practical AI efficiency

Define the task and acceptance criteria before comparing completion time, rework and usefulness on equivalent tasks. Record sample size, tool versions and human intervention. Distinguish drafts, completed tasks and usable outputs. More generated text does not establish time savings. These are evaluation methods, not measured efficiency gains claimed by this project.
